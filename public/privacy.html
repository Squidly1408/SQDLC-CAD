Privacy Policy

SQDLC-CAD - Last updated 31 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information SQDLC-CAD, a free, browser-based 3D modeling / CAD tool, collects, how it's used, and your choices. SQDLC-CAD is an independent project operated by Lucas Newman (robotduck961@gmail.com). This is a plain-language policy for a small independent project, not a substitute for legal advice.

What we collect

  • None. As described in the README, there is no sign-up, no account, and no server - everything you model runs and is stored entirely on your own machine, in your browser.

Third-party services

We don't use any third-party services that receive your personal data.

How we use information

We use the information above only to operate, maintain, and improve SQDLC-CAD - for example, to let you sign in, save your data, process payments you've requested, or show the app's live status. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at robotduck961@gmail.com. If SQDLC-CAD offers an account, you can typically delete your own data from within the app; otherwise, email us and we'll action your request as soon as reasonably possible.

Data retention & security

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by our service providers' own retention settings (e.g. Firebase). We take reasonable steps to protect data but, as with any online service, we can't guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

SQDLC-CAD is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we'll remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly. Continued use of SQDLC-CAD after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email robotduck961@gmail.com.
